Who we are

Antonios Paraschidis

Antonios Paraschidis was born in the city of Drama, Greece, son of Symeon and Vasiliki. He was educated at the Economic Lyceum and the Boys' Lyceum of Drama, schools closely tied to the history of education in the city.

The Boys' Lyceum (today the 1st General Lyceum of Drama) is one of the most historic and imposing school buildings in Greece. It stands on Katakouzinou 12 at the north-western edge of the city; construction began in 1927–1928, under the government of Eleftherios Venizelos, in a neo-Byzantine style designed by G. Pantzaris. It is known for its vast, lush, dreamlike courtyard and its characteristic E-shaped plan.

He later relocated to Thessaloniki, where he began and expanded his knowledge of furniture design, balcony and stair design, the architecture of rooms and the architecture of lighting. He travelled to Italy, where he studied emblematic buildings and gardens, and collaborated with professionals from Germany. He has managed construction and design projects in almost every city in Greece.

He has designed solutions for private condominiums in Santorini and for businesses of every kind: from aesthetic hair salons to multi-level constructions and professional dining atmospheres for some of the most exquisite stores.

We provide high-level professional proposals, able to handle Interior Architecture and Design with an inventive and critical spirit — balancing Science and Art, and the practical side of Design. Our understanding of design and its practical applications, combined with decades of experience, lets us guarantee results that have impressed, and will impress you too.
